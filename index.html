Research

Research Intern

GIPSA-Lab (CNRS), Inria Grenoble Rhone-Alpes and CHU Grenoble Alpes

March 2026 to present

Master's thesis on automatic quantification of skeletal muscle in pediatric MRI. I measured how far SegmentAnyMuscle, a foundation model pretrained on adult MRI, transfers to children, and characterised its failure modes along acquisition, anatomical coverage and pathology on a clinical cohort of nine children.

I benchmarked three annotation-efficient adaptation routes: parameter-efficient fine-tuning with a frozen Vision Transformer backbone, adapters and a mixture-of-experts head; a 2-D nnU-Net trained from scratch, which reached Dice 0.94 on a held-out severe-pathology thigh; and self-supervised masked-image pretraining on 123 unlabelled series. Labels came from a human-in-the-loop workflow where clinicians corrected predictions rather than redrawing them, producing 285 expert-reviewed slices across 30 volumes.

I also tested MedGemma, a medical vision-language model, as a controller for pixel-level annotation, through tool-driven brush control and through function calling, to find where a general medical VLM stops being dependable for localisation.

Projects
